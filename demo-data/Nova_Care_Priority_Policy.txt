NOVA CARE PRIORITY SUPPORT POLICY
Version 3.1 — Demonstration knowledge source

1. Critical safety incidents
Battery swelling, smoke, exposed electrical components, or extreme overheating are classified as P0 safety incidents. Customers must be instructed to stop using and charging the device immediately.

2. Priority replacement eligibility
Verified Nova X1 battery swelling within 24 months of purchase qualifies for a no-cost priority replacement. No inspection fee applies. The delivery address must be confirmed before execution.

3. Human approval boundary
The support intelligence system may verify eligibility and prepare the replacement. A human or the customer must explicitly approve the action before the replacement order is created.

4. Service commitment
Priority replacements in Bengaluru target delivery within two to three business days. A carrier escalation must be created when the commitment is exceeded by more than 12 hours.
